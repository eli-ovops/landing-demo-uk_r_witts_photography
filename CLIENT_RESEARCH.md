`H0.5: CONDITIONAL — meaningful static opportunity exists, but commercial basis needs confirmation before Demo authorisation.`

It does not tell a first-time visitor what photography work is available to commission, which work is most representative, who it is for, where it is offered, or what happens after the first contact. It would be inappropriate to build a generic commercial-photography page by inventing services or treating political/public-event material as permission to position the business politically. The concept basis must stay neutral, evidence-bound and restricted to public work that genuinely supports the confirmed commission category.
