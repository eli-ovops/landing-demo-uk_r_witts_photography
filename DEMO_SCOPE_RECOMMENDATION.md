# Demo scope recommendation — R Witts Photography LTD

## Conclusion: CONDITIONAL — do not authorise Demo now

A £600 static single page is technically appropriate only if the page represents a verifiable commission offer. The current public record supports a portfolio/contact upgrade but does not yet prove that this is a meaningful commercial acquisition use case, nor which work may accurately anchor it.

## If the listed conditions are met later

`Demo Effort: MEDIUM`  
`Target production time: 30–60 minutes`  
`Actual Demo Minutes: NOT APPLICABLE — no Demo authorised or made`

### Shown in a tightly bounded concept

- Business name, a verified commission category, and one direct email CTA.
- A short, evidence-supported reason a visitor might commission the work.
- 2–3 verified service labels only after they are publicly substantiated.
- Selected-work links that return to existing Pixieset galleries; do not recreate ecommerce or a client proofing system.
- One factual current-operating signal and an evidence-safe region statement if available.

### Reserved for any final delivery

- Full client-approved copy, complete portfolio architecture and complete service range.
- Customer-approved imagery, permissions, credits, testimonials, client logos, awards and precise coverage information.
- Domain/hosting, SEO metadata, analytics, privacy/legal content and all production accessibility/browser QA.
- Any booking, proofing, image sales or payment workflow, which must remain external or separately scoped.

### Explicit exclusions

No CMS, database, login, customer gallery/proofing, self-hosted booking, ecommerce, payment, inventory, server-side form, tracking, account access, deployment or maintenance commitment.

## Purchase-signal interpretation

There is evidence of an active incorporated photography practice and current publicly visible work. These are at most weak presentation-investment / operating signals. They do not prove £600 affordability, a website budget, a desire to buy, demand for more clients or a current trigger. `NO CLEAR TRIGGER FOUND.`

## Stop conditions

Stop rather than make a generic portfolio if the only available evidence remains event chronology and general SIC codes; if rights cannot be resolved; if the commercial offer requires invention; or if the evidence-selection work pushes honest production beyond the 30–60 minute boundary.
