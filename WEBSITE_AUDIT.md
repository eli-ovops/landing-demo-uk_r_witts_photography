## Information-expression gaps and visitor impact

| Current reproducible observation | Visitor impact | Static, evidence-safe response if later authorised |
|---|---|---|
| Hero is only company name plus phone/email. | A prospective commissioner cannot infer what can be commissioned before scanning imagery. | Name a verified commission category and a neutral one-line positioning statement; do not infer it from the company name. |
| Gallery titles are date/event led, with the most prominent work about public events and reportage. | The portfolio conveys photographic capability but not a defined offer, target client or desired type of enquiry. | Curate only evidence-supported selected work behind an agreed/verified category; no political framing or invented commercial niche. |
| No visible coverage/region statement on the official profile. | Visitors cannot tell whether Cardiff, South Wales, London or another area is actually served. | Use only a directly verified coverage statement; otherwise omit it. |
| Phone/email are visible but not contextualised as an enquiry path. | A visitor has no stated next action or expectation after contact. | A simple direct `mailto:` CTA can be used after a confirmed purpose; no form or booking system is needed. |
| There is no evident trust/proof module beyond the work itself. | Visitors cannot distinguish documentary portfolio history from an active commission service. | Use only verified proof such as dated current work or a factual company identity; do not invent reviews, client logos, credentials or outcomes. |
